How the plan works

Reorder point = sales per day × (lead time + safety days). Target = sales per day × (lead + safety + review days).

Branches holding more than 1.5× their target donate stock to branches at or below their reorder point. Only the remaining shortfall is bought, rounded up to the supplier's minimum order, most urgent first until the budget runs out.
